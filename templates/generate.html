Generation Process
  • 1
    Query Understanding: Your topic is analyzed to understand the requirements
  • 2
    Document Retrieval: If RAG is enabled, relevant document chunks are retrieved
  • 3
    Content Generation: The selected LLM model generates the content with appropriate context
  • 4
    Validation: The content is validated for consistency, relevance, tone, and compliance
  • 5
    Results: The generated content is presented with validation scores and feedback
